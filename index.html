Develop your coaching by understanding your voice.

The Coach Analyst supports coaches by analysing communication from real training sessions and matches. Using audio only, we help coaches reflect on how they speak, question, and intervene — without judgement, scoring, or player analysis.

How it works

1. Context first

We start with a short conversation about who you coach, what the session was about, and what you’d like to improve.

2. You send session audio

This can be a phone recording, voice note, or camera audio. You coach exactly as normal.

3. Voice analysis

We look at patterns such as intervention frequency, talk time, questions, directness, and tone — always with uncertainty.

4. Reflective discussion

You receive a simple summary and a short conversation to explore what it means in your context.

5. One focus habit

You choose one small, realistic thing to experiment with next time.

Who this is for

This is for:
  • Grassroots coaches
  • Volunteer and parent coaches
  • Academy coaches
  • Coach developers
This is not:
  • Player performance analysis
  • Coaching assessment or appraisal
  • Scoring or ranking coaches
  • Something shared without consent
